How the yard works

Read each track from left to right. The buffer is fixed at the left. Only the wagon at the exposed right end can move.

Choose its track, then another track with a free space. The wagon joins the right end there. Each transfer counts as one move.

Match the small Dispatch order under every track, including empty ones. You may use a correct track as temporary parking.

Click a selected track again to cancel. Tab and Enter/Space work too. Undo reopens a completed dispatch.

About the little hints

A hint marks a source and destination without moving a wagon. Follow it, then ask again for another shortest step.

Every legal transfer has an inverse. A breadth-first search from the dispatch order finds the exact fewest transfers from your current yard. Detours and undo are welcome.
