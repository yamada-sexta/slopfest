Your brush

Longer periods space the waves farther apart. Change the period, then let the old ripples settle.

Keyboard in the tank: arrows move one cell, Shift + arrows move eight. Enter uses the brush; Space runs or pauses. Drawing pauses the water.

What the water knows

Each cell feels its four neighbors. Two previous states carry the motion forward, with a little damping. Barriers hold the surface at zero; waves bend around openings and reflect at edges.

Orange is positive displacement; blue is negative. Brightness is boosted so small waves stay visible. This is a small grid experiment, not a calibrated water simulation. Hiding the page pauses it. Nothing is stored or uploaded.
