How to read the walk

The cursor points at the next tile. Step executes it, changes the pocket or direction, and moves one cell. Back restores the previous recorded state and retracts its output. Walk follows this trace; at the end, Walk starts it again.
Focus the map: ← / → steps, Home / End jumps, Space walks or pauses. Editing pauses and disables the trace controls until Load succeeds. Hiding the page pauses the walk. No edits or input are stored or uploaded.
