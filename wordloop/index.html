For example, a window of three beads slides around the circle. Each move keeps two symbols and brings in one more. Words can overlap, so we don’t need to write every word separately.

How the circle is made

Imagine a town for each possible suffix. Appending a symbol follows a road to the next suffix. The builder takes every road once, then joins the little circuits into one loop.

Afterward, a separate audit reads every circular window and counts it. Nothing missing. Nothing repeated. One bead per possible word is the smallest a circular ribbon can be: each bead starts just one window.

Keyboard: focus the ring. ← / → steps, Home / End jumps to the first / last bead, and Space walks or pauses. Tap the ring near a bead to select it. A lap stops where it began; pause or step to inspect a word. Hiding the page pauses a walk.
