How to play the gardener

The gardener answers immediately. It chooses the smallest winning cut when one exists; otherwise it takes the smallest legal branch. It uses no randomness.

Against the gardener, undo takes back your cut and its reply together. With two people, undo takes back one cut. A cleared grove can also be undone.
