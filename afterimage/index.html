How the machine works

Move with the arrows or WASD. Each move advances every echo one step. Space waits one beat. A blocked move takes no time.

Press R to rewind. Your walk becomes an echo, and you return to the start. Echoes replay their recordings, then stay at their final square. They can overlap and walk through you.

Doors open when every matching plate is held on the beat you enter. You have three recordings and 64 beats per walk. Z undoes your last step. Reset clears all recordings.
