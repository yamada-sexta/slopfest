Mint frames mark a smallest set of windows to knock, in any order. The plan updates after each move.

A knock travels one step.

Knock a window to flip it and its neighbors above, below, left, and right. Lit turns dark; dark turns lit. Edges stop the cross. The goal is all windows dark.

Dotted frames preview the windows your next knock would flip. ON/OFF labels show their current state. A hint chooses a window; you make the knock.

Why the hint is shortest

Two knocks in the same place cancel. So a quiet plan never needs the same window twice. The helper checks every valid odd/even knock pattern and chooses one with the fewest marked windows.

A shortest route from where you are now may differ from the one at the start. Any one of its marked windows moves you one knock closer.

Six prepared rooms grow from a three-by-three landing to a five-by-five building. Every starting room has a quiet plan. Undo retracts a knock, even after the room is solved. Reset room starts this room over.

A little help stays nearby.

Hints and the whole-plan view are always available. Once every light is out, choose the next room, undo, or start over. There is no timer, failure state, or saved score. Switching rooms clears the attempt and hides the plan.

Keyboard: Tab into the building, arrow keys choose a window, Home/End select the first/last, and Enter or Space knocks. Postcards save the current lights and counts without hint or preview frames. Everything stays in this tab; reloading starts at the first room.
