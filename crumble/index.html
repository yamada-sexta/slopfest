Four is a crowd.

A ready cell gives one grain north, east, south, and west. At an open edge, a grain leaves the postcard forever. Nothing wraps around. A wave uses the heights at its start; fresh arrivals wait for the next wave. Tall heaps can topple several times in one wave.

A memory of falling.

The work layer counts every individual topple since the original pour. Its colors use a logarithmic scale, so quiet places can share the page with busy ones. Saving an SVG keeps the current layer and counts, without the selection box.
