Focus the cloth: arrows move one tile; Enter turns it. In Follow mode, Enter picks the first arc and Space picks the second. Z undoes a turn.

How the loom joins up

Each square has two quarter-circle threads, with a thread end at every side. Neighbors always meet. Threads leaving an edge return from the opposite edge, like a cloth whose sides are stitched together.

A winding loop makes a net trip around a seam. Crossing a seam and coming back does not count as winding. The inspector counts horizontal and vertical trips; tracing direction is arbitrary. Every tile turn can merge, split, or reroute loops. Colors help you follow them, but are reassigned after a turn.

No crossing goes over or under: this is a tiling toy, not a knot simulator. Nothing is stored or uploaded. SVG saves the current colors, grid setting, and highlighted loop, without the keyboard cursor.
