    The trick

    No undo stack.
    Just an opposite.

    Adding becomes subtracting. A left rotation turns right. Multiplying by 13 becomes multiplying by 197, because 13 × 197 wraps around to 1.

    Backward steps run those opposites in reverse order. The machine calculates its way home from the current bytes.

    A pocket guide to the language

    Registers are a b c d. All values wrap at 256.

    add / sub / xor a b
    Mix another register or a literal byte into a.
    mul a 13
    Multiply by an odd byte. Even multipliers lose information.
    rol / ror a 3
    Rotate 0–7 bits left or right.
    not a · swap a b
    Flip every bit, or exchange two different registers.
    repeat 3 … end
    Repeat a block 1–32 times. # begins a comment.

    Source registers must differ from targets. Up to four nested repeats and 4,096 expanded steps.
