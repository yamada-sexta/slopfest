About the light

Beams pass through each other. A mirror affects every beam that reaches it. Split-color stamps need both lamps, even if their beams travel in opposite directions. A loop keeps shining until you redirect it.

Hints highlight one mirror on a shortest route from your current arrangement to a solution. No turn is made for you. Progress lives only in this page; choose any postcard at any time.
